Scheduling by Navigation

Scheduling languages such as Halide are pervasive in domain-specific optimizing compilers. Authoring good schedules, however, is difficult due to the complexity of modern workloads and the vastness of the scheduling space. In this project we use Honeybee, an interactive program synthesis tool with strong guarantees on both the interaction and the resulting schedule, to explore methods that scaffold the schedule writing process.
